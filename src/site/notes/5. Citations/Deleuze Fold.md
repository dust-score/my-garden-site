Deleuze, Gilles. _The Fold: Leibniz and the Baroque_. Translated by Tom Conley. _Yale French Studies_ 80 (1991): 228. "The ichnographic chart of the Universe, the relation of all-to-one and one-to-all is the systematic theme of Leibnizianism and of this work," Serres, II, 620. Cf. _Théodicée_, §416. Christiane Frémont has shown in what way the story of Sextus is a "founding narrative" of the Roman empire, in "Trois fictions sur le problème du mal," in René Girard, _Le problème du mal_ (Paris: Grasset, 1982).
