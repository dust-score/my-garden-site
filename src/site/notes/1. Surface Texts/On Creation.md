_[[3. Concepts/Eyn Sof\|Eyn Sof]]_ intended to experience Godself. To do so, it had to become less and also more than Godself: allow for something that was not God, that would nevertheless become _through_ God, as God was all that was. If God was infinity, how could anything be that was not God? God contracted, and this was _[[3. Concepts/tsimtsum-nahman\|tsimtsum]]_. Through tsimtsum, God made space for creation. This did not alter the infinite nature of G-D. Within the vacated space ([[3. Concepts/The Empty Space\|ḥalal panui]]) was left the reshimu, the impression or trace, the subtle remnant of divine light, which holds the potential for creation, like the trace of ink on a paper after the letters have been erased. _[[3. Concepts/reshimu\|Reshimu]]_ becomes the substance of the vessels, which are formed of the trace — the _[[3. Concepts/shevirat ha-kelim\|shevirat ha-kelim]]_ [vessels] which are to hold Ein Sof's light. Therefore, the _kelim_ are formed from the _reshimu_ [trace] of divinity within the _[[3. Concepts/The Empty Space\|The Empty Space]]_ [space] of its absence, and from it the vessels were made. Afterward the _qaw_ [line - the controlled reintroduction of divinity] came into it and from it was made the _aẓmut_ [essence]. This is the difference between the essence and the vessels, for the vessels are from the aspect of the contraction [mi-beḥinat ha-ṣimṣum], and the essence is the secret of Ein Sof, which enters into the vacated space [ḥalal ha-meṣumṣam]_._ This is the bridging of in/finitude; [[2. Main Theoretical Pillars/wolfson-retroactive-not-yet\|as Wolfson states in The Retroactive Not Yet]], “the main goal of the withdrawal is to produce the vessels that will reveal the light by concealing it, since the nonmanifest cannot be manifest without being [[3. Concepts/On Obfuscation\|occluded]].” This is a notation of [[2. Main Theoretical Pillars/Entropic Love Letter\|the necessity of limitation]], ergo finitude, in the creation of the world.

_"The potential for finitude is thus coiled within the folds of infinity – it could not be otherwise because the inclusivity of the infinite is such that it must possess even the capacity to be exclusive, the capacity to be less than infinite."_[^1]

These partzufim are not mere attributes but dynamic, interacting personalities, capable of "enclothing" themselves within one another, as a soul becoming enclothed in a body. It is through their interactions that the work of [[1. Surface Texts/TIKKUN\|TIKKUN]] proceeds: the scattered divine sparks from the shattered vessels of Tohu, now trapped within the lower worlds of Beri'ah (Creation), Yetzirah (Formation), and Asiyah (Action), are gradually elevated and restored to their source. This elevation is not accomplished by divine fiat but through human agency: the performance of mitzvot (commandments), mystical kavanot (intentions), and the rectification of one's own soul, which is mirrored in the divine realms above.

[^1]:  The Retroactive Not Yet, Elliot Wolfson, pg 49 ; in Linear Circularity and Kabbalistic Temporality
