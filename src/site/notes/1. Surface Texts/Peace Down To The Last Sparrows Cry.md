[[1. Surface Texts/Jobcenter Texts/Love\|Let there be no heaven and no earth]],
peace down to the last sparrow’s cry,
the dearly beloved white horses are also
on speaking terms with eternity,<br><br>
and all because you would not pay
me as your prophet my just deserts
and all because not a penny
of boiling money comes from, goes to, me;<br><br>  
Yes, all stands in the autism of ages
when every greeting is a coin high in the sky
shimmering over the madness of your tides
when the lost white horse surges from the breaker,  
and all stands as before, my prophetic calling  
scuds from the molten letter Gimmel.<br><br>
<div style="text-align: right;">In the scuds of the molten letter Gimmel <br> 
I sought well-being, and found it not:<br>  
the Succot branches under an open sky <br> 
the language that no hot iron breaks<br><br><br>  
the foaming on the tongue, transfigured<br>  
like the burden of memory into prayer<br>
the bitter herbs in the palace<br>  
as in the hut of authority<br><br><br>  
the bitter peace on the kerbstone<br>  
torn from side to side by the host people<br>  
the nebbish in pyjamas in the madhouse<br>  
roared at by the conscience of clouds<br>  
and ringed by fire in the eyes, otherness<br>  
of the faithful, death in the rafters.<br><br><br>
Amos Weisz, Worksongs</div>
