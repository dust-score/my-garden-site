[[1. Surface Texts/Jobcenter Texts/Love\|Let there be no heaven and no earth]],<br>  
peace down to the last sparrow’s cry,<br>  
the dearly beloved white horses are also<br>  
on speaking terms with eternity,<br><br><br>  
and all because you would not pay<br>  
me as your prophet my just deserts<br>  
and all because not a penny<br>  
of boiling money comes from, goes to, me;<br><br><br>  
Yes, all stands in the autism of ages<br>  
when every greeting is a coin high in the sky<br>  
shimmering over the madness of your tides<br>  
when the lost white horse surges from the breaker,<br>  
and all stands as before, my prophetic calling<br>  
scuds from the molten letter Gimmel.<br><br><br>
<div style="text-align: right;">In the scuds of the molten letter Gimmel <br> 
I sought well-being, and found it not:<br>  
the Succot branches under an open sky <br> 
the language that no hot iron breaks<br><br><br>  
the foaming on the tongue, transfigured<br>  
like the burden of memory into prayer<br>
the bitter herbs in the palace<br>  
as in the hut of authority<br><br><br>  
the bitter peace on the kerbstone<br>  
torn from side to side by the host people<br>  
the nebbish in pyjamas in the madhouse<br>  
roared at by the conscience of clouds<br>  
and ringed by fire in the eyes, otherness<br>  
of the faithful, death in the rafters.<br><br><br>
Amos Weisz, Worksongs</div>
