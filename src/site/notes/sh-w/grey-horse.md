In the grey infinite marshalling flocks of crumbs and nail clippings with a lasso from the back of my [[sh-w/dirty horse\|dirty horse]]:

Yesterday sleep deprived delirious I rolled past the lake on my other horse. Like mercury it shone in the dim afternoon and cleared the pressure from my head. Up I went to the road and there lay the whole city like mercury, wet and gleaming. Then I felt the whole city could clear my head.

It’s the [[sh-w/gnawing\|gnawing]] stomach of the world. Pre-packaged colour packs offset the grey linoleum.

When I first dropped onto the scene of those linoleum hallways the Germans could not hold themselves from tumbling from the doorways; *look, a whitey!* they called softly to one another, and each responded tenderly.

The idiot already balding, the TV youth, the dreamer distracted by mundane physical truth, the slick marketeer. And, my Master’s [[sh-w/voice\|voice]], and me with head cocked, seeking Him and yet not finding Him. Falling asleep in my skull while I push the horse from door to door.

[[sh-w/Remember\|Remember]] that evening? I knelt down at each door, and at each door D pushed together a pile of teeth.
