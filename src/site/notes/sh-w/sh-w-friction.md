There is no acceleration in the Jobcenter. Jobcenter is friction ontology. It’s not quite care and it’s not quite neglect. Jobcenter is maintenance velocity. Jobcenter is material coil and me, circling each countervailing spiral force.  
  
Jobcenter is paternalism, grey and wipeable, just enough or never enough, Jobcenter makes sense. From the cellar to the lift to the 5th floor, through myriad offices, to the back lift, to the sixth floor, back around, to the back lift, to the ninth floor, back around, and then, open a window and climb the roof. Up above, look out! Can you see the passage of [[sh-w/sh-w-power\|effortless]] power, that which requires no permission at all?

Dusty is neither forgiveness nor atonement. Dusty does not repair, because nothing is broken. She is that which turns power inwards. Dusty is inward-folding, implication. The incompatibility of the world clangs behind my eyelids; Dusty kisses unspent tears. Dusty is not ignorance nor compensation. Dusty is the [[sh-w/sh-w-love\|secret]] third thing that makes all the difference.
