eenie weenie hapless creatures, drawn on GDR Randlochkarte (data entry card) sourced from the paper bin of the Jobcenter in Berlin Lichtenberg.

The building was, before the reunification of west and east Germany,
HQ of the GDR secret police, aka the Stasi (Staatssicherheit).

The creatures enjoy that, in the continuation of the materials of e.g. bureaucracy of surveillance, or coercive care between systems, there remains
something tangible and close-by.

They span the distance between the nonverbal reality of the material and the hyperverbal non-reality of narrative manoeuvers, and they are having a great time of it.
