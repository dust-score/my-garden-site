Power gurgles down fluorescent halls adjoining [[sh-w/sh-w-friction\|rectangular prism]] one to the next, neither culminating nor abating but only trundling, steadily, held back. Jobcenter aesthetosphere: easeless, banal, compensatory. 

For every grey surface there is neon, trinket, flippant 3D, that brakes power and ties it up; am held hostage by ugly square paintings row by row. 

The rectangular prism neither diffracts nor [[sh-w/sh-w-power\|focuses]] but maintains: [[sh-w/Jobcenter eschaton\|Jobcenter eschaton]]. No unfolding but [[sh-w/sh-w-love\|infolding]], sheath by sheath.

It does still fascinate me, though not to a degree of enthusiasm, that everything that logs is rectangular, and I amble through, excessive beyond consequence. 

Jobcenter is [[sh-w/mystery\|mystery]] of the banal, rectangular prisms, log information. When God made the Jobcenter he told his bureaucrats, a rectangular prism neither diffracts nor focuses, but maintains. Then he giggled in Dusty’s ear: there’s always some diffraction round the edges.
