Here, then, I find it gainful to employ Agamben’s secularised messianicity, the mode of agency of the subject of _bare life_, the symmetric opposition to _sovereign power_. If sovereign power is that which decides on the establishment of the state of exception,  messianicity is the implementation within the self or oppositional community of an interiorised _aufhebung_: the moment in which something does not become, but is, possible.
